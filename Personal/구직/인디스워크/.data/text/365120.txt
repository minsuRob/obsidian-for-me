Position Overview
MangoBoost is actively seeking skilled DV (Design Verification) engineers to join our Seoul office in Korea for IP/FPGA/SoC (System-on-Chip) developments. The role involves a wide range of responsibilities, including verifying in-house accelerator IPs and performing comprehensive verification for complex systems encompassing commercial IPs and in-house IPs. DV engineers need to understand the functionalities of the DPU IPs and construct robust verification environments, develop workflows and verification methodologies, all activities are aimed to develop bug-free hardware IPs to make MangoBoost DPU solutions a lot more valuable.
Responsibilities & Opportunities
Lead comprehensive system-level software/hardware verification and troubleshooting for MangoBoost DPUs using RTL simulation, ensuring robustness and reliability
Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams (IP Design, Software, Architecture, SoC, etc.) to meticulously validate the functionality and performance of DPU systems and applications
Evaluate system workflow and performance, providing recommendations for enhancements based on user feedback, complaints, and bug reports from other teams
Opportunity to spearhead the development and implementation of innovative HW-focused verification methodologies to enhance DPU product quality and efficiency
Engage in continuous learning and professional development to stay updated with the latest DV techniques and technologies
Required Qualifications
Master’s degree holders may apply as entry-level candidates.
Bachelor, Master, or PhD in Electrical or Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related field
Experience in RTL simulation with Verilog/System Verilog
Experience in digital HW verification flow
Preferred Qualifications
2+ year industry experience in performance verification (measuring and analyzing DUT performance metrics)
Ability to write UVM testbench code from scratch
Familiarity with protocols and standards in the AMBA, Ethernet, PCIe and NVMe domains
Ability to establish comprehensive and concrete verification plans
Working experiences in coverage driven constraint random verification
Ability to model a system with OOP (Object-Oriented Programming) paradigm
Strong background on computer architecture and operating system
Experience in verifying ARM-based SoC scenarios with large-scale master/slave connections over NoC bus, including QoS behavior verification, using RTL simulation.
