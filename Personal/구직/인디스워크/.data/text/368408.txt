About Cutback
Cutback is building an AI that understands speech, vision, and context in raw footage, organizes it, and generates rough cuts. We are cutting out the repetitive grind of the editing room so creators can focus purely on storytelling. The Challenge
We are building an AI that edits video. It turns out to be one of the hardest problems in applied ML, as the craft of good video editing resists automation in ways most problems don’t:
Massive Scale:
Inputs range from hundreds of gigabytes to multiple terabytes per project.
No Ground Truth:
The craft of editing barely exists as structured data anywhere on the internet.
Zero Margin for Error:
A single-frame error (0.03s) is immediately obvious and jarring to viewers.
Subjective Success:
There is no single correct answer. The exact same footage can become high-energy entertainment or a grounded documentary depending entirely on intent.
Solving this requires engineers who think beyond conventional software boundaries. You will touch video-understanding pipelines, AI agents, evaluation framework, and core infrastructure – whatever it takes to make the AI better.
