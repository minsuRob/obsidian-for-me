Overview
Octave is hiring a Software Consultant (Technical Support Engineer) to support Octave Attune EAM customers across Korea and APAC.
Key Responsibilities
Troubleshoot complex EAM application, database, interface, and system issues
Work with development/product teams and keep customers updated
Translate maintenance/operations problems into technical solutions
Maintain documentation, knowledge base, and support processes
Use AI tools to improve troubleshooting and documentation
Required Skills
Strong SQL skills and relational database knowledge
Experience with Oracle and/or SQL Server
Understanding of application servers such as JBoss/Apache
Knowledge of REST/SOAP APIs, SSO, XML/JSON
Confidence using AI tools in daily work
Fluent Korean and professional English
Preferred Experience
EAM/CMMS experience such as HxGN/Infor EAM, Maximo, or SAP PM
Background in manufacturing, heavy industry, power, petrochemical, utilities, etc.
Knowledge of maintenance, reliability, MRO inventory, and asset management
Skills in Python, GIS, cloud/SaaS, log analytics, or CRM are a plus
Ideal Candidate
Self-starter with strong learning mindset
2+ years in technical support or related industry experience
Degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, or related field
Engineering background is highly preferred
*This is a 1-year contract position with the possibility of conversion to permanent employment depending on performance and business needs.
