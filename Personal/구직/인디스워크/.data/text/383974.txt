Job Description
The Process Engineer is responsible for developing, optimizing, and sustaining semiconductor manufacturing processes to ensure stable production, high yield, and high-quality products. This role works closely with manufacturing, equipment, and engineering teams to support process improvement and technology deployment.
Responsibilities
Develop, optimize, and maintain semiconductor manufacturing processes
Support process integration, yield improvement, and defect reduction activities
Plan, execute, and analyze DOE and engineering runs
Monitor inline data and identify process issues and root causes
Collaborate with equipment, device, and manufacturing teams for process improvements
Support process transfer, qualification, and production ramp-up activities
Qualifications
Bachelor’s degree or higher in Engineering (Electrical, Electronic, Materials, Chemical, or related fields)
Experience in semiconductor process engineering or manufacturing environment preferred
Understanding of semiconductor fabrication processes and basic device physics
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Ability to work effectively in cross-functional teams
Good communication skills in English
