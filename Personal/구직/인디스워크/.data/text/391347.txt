THE ROLE:
You will be passionate about graphics and gaming and will partner directly with game developers to help them launch their titles and get the best possible performance out of AMD hardware. You will be a member of a core team of incredibly talented industry specialists and will work with the very latest hardware and software technology.
THE PERSON:
You will be passionate about real-time graphics and GPU technology. You will also possess the communication skills to act as a trusted technical point of contact for game studios and be comfortable driving sophisticated issues to resolution across external partners and teams within AMD.
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:
Work directly with game developers to optimize their titles and bring them successfully to market on AMD hardware
Serve as the primary technical point of contact for partner game studios
Apply a data-driven approach to analyzing and improving rendering performance
Debug and resolve graphics and performance issues spanning game engines, graphics APIs, and the GPU
Work with AMD’s architecture specialists to influence and improve future products
Stay informed of graphics, gaming, and hardware trends, especially in rendering and GPU architecture
PREFERRED EXPERIENCE:
Strong C/C++ programming background with a keen attention to detail
Solid understanding of modern graphics APIs (Direct3D 12 required; Vulkan a plus)
Knowledge of rendering techniques, graphics pipelines, and modern game engines (Unreal Engine a strong plus)
Familiarity with GPU internals, ISA, and GPU profiling/optimization
Console and other platform development experience a plus
Strong communication, presentation, and negotiation skills
Native or near-native written and spoken Korean is mandatory as you will be communicating with external customers
ACADEMIC CREDENTIALS:
Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or equivalent
