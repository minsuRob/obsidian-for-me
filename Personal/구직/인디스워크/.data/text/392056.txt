About the Role
We are looking for an Autonomy Software Engineer to help build and scale core autonomy capabilities. This is a product engineering role for people who enjoy shipping real systems: designing clean software, developing new features, improving reliability, and delivering measurable impact in real operations. The role is best suited for early-career to mid-level engineers who are ready to grow their technical ownership while working on real-world autonomy systems. Experience in robotics or autonomous driving is a plus, not a strict requirement.
What You Will Do
Build and improve core autonomy functionality in planning, control, and estimation pipelines
Design, implement, and maintain robust Python and C++ software for production use
Help translate product and operational needs into practical technical solutions
Improve system reliability, performance, and maintainability across modules
Investigate complex issues using logs, simulation, and operational data, and contribute to durable fixes
Develop test and validation workflows to support safe and confident releases
Contribute to engineering quality through design reviews, code reviews, and better development practices
Required Qualifications
Strong software engineering fundamentals and hands-on C++ development experience
Solid grasp of linear algebra, calculus, and geometry, and the ability to translate mathematical concepts into working software
Experience developing non-trivial software systems through professional work, research, or substantial projects
Strong problem-solving and debugging skills, including the ability to investigate issues systematically
Experience writing clean, testable, maintainable code
Effective collaboration and communication skills in team environments
Willingness to take responsibility for assigned problems and follow them through implementation and validation
Preferred Qualifications
Robotics, autonomous driving, or other real-time/safety-critical system experience
Practical understanding of planning, control, and/or state estimation systems
Experience with simulation-based development and validation
Experience with Linux-based development and CI/CD workflows
Experience owning a software feature or component from implementation through testing and integration
Leveling (Junior/Mid)
Junior-level engineers are expected to contribute to features and modules with guidance, while developing stronger system-level understanding and gradually taking on more ownership
Mid-level engineers are expected to independently own features or modules, make sound implementation decisions, and contribute to technical design
Why Join Us
Work on autonomy software that controls individual vehicles and entire fleets in real-world deployment, where your decisions directly affect safety and performance
Collaborate with a strong cross-functional team solving practical, high-value problems
Grow from hands-on execution to larger technical ownership over time
