General Summary:
We are building next-generation on-device Agentic AI technologies that provide intelligent, personalized, and context-aware assistance while preserving efficiency and privacy on edge devices. Our research focuses on enabling AI systems to understand user context, learn from interactions, maintain intelligent memory, and perform complex reasoning through efficient on-device multimodal models.
We are based in Seoul and collaborate closely with research teams across multiple global sites. We are looking for research interns interested in developing novel theories, algorithms, and systems in the following areas for Agentic AI:
Long-term memory and memory-augmented AI systems.
Personalization, User context understanding, multimodal user modeling
Multimodal generative models (LLM, LVM) and Foundation models
Reinforcement learning, Continual learning, Knowledge distillation for SLM, SMM
Hybrid agents, Privacy, Data construction
The technologies developed during the internship may be deployed on commercial mobile and edge devices powered by Qualcomm platforms. Research outcomes are expected to contribute to state-of-the-art AI technologies and may lead to publications in top-tier AI conference.
