What you’ll build
We’re looking for a Technology associate consultant for a new project – Support trainee and program by teaching how to get the most out of development.
SAP is working in an exciting, ever evolving market. Our goal is to show trainees how to get maximum usage and fun out of SAP software solutions. We do this by providing knowledge transfer in a way that fit the need of our trainees.
As the SAP Technology associate consultant, you will:
• Learn about relevant advanced SAP development language (ABAP, Cloud ABAP and SAP UI5)
• Play the role of delivering basic training in field of development
• Support training contents design and development (Main Language: ABAP, Cloud ABAP and SAP UI5)
• Support technical assistance for trainee
• Talk to external consultants who are instructors or mentors.
• Class Administration and Class Management
Where you belong
At SAP, you will be part of an inclusive, global community of technology consulting professionals dedicated to translating complex business needs into innovative, scalable technology solutions. Together, we foster a culture where deep technical expertise, strategic thinking, and client focus combine to define IT architectures, drive transformation, and deliver outstanding technology outcomes for our customers and partners.
