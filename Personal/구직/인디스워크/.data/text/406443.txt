Here are some available intern and graduate positions on offer:
Process Engineers
Advance next-generation semiconductor technology by running experiments, process qualifications, and hardware evaluations that help bring innovative products to market.
Solve real-world engineering challenges by troubleshooting advanced equipment, analyzing process data, and driving improvements in manufacturing performance and reliability.
Gain hands-on experience with cutting-edge tools while collaborating with engineers, technicians, and operators to develop and optimize semiconductor processes.
Make a measurable impact by supporting customer demonstrations, technology evaluations, and continuous improvement initiatives that shape the future of the semiconductor industry.
Work hands-on with advanced deposition technologies like ALD, PEALD, PECVD, and epitaxy to develop next-gen devices
Software Engineers
Develop advanced software for industry-leading semiconductor technologies.
See your code drive the operation of some of the most sophisticated semiconductor equipment in the world.
Collaborate with a global team of talented software engineers.
Contribute to the next generation of semiconductor equipment control software and automation technologies.
Mechanical Engineers
Design and engineer semiconductor products using mechanical design, simulation, and analytical problem-solving techniques.
Build detailed 3D CAD models and engineering drawings while supporting prototype development and production readiness.
Investigate technical challenges through troubleshooting and failure analysis to improve product performance and reliability.
Partner with engineering, sourcing, manufacturing, suppliers, and customers to deliver innovative solutions from concept through production.
Electrical Engineers
Design the technology behind advanced semiconductor equipment, creating electrical systems, controls, and hardware that power next-generation chip manufacturing.
Turn ideas into reality by developing sub-assemblies, integrating system components, and customizing solutions to meet customer and product requirements.
Drive innovation and continuous improvement through design reviews, engineering change projects, testing, and performance validation of new hardware solutions.
Collaborate across global teams to solve complex technical challenges, support product development, and bring cutting-edge technologies from concept to production.
QA Engineers
Work with advanced semiconductor technology, troubleshooting software, hardware, and control system challenges that impact cutting-edge manufacturing equipment.
Drive product quality and reliability by testing new software releases, identifying bugs, validating fixes, and enhancing automated regression testing.
Gain hands-on experience with both software simulators and real production tools while collaborating closely with global engineering, QA, and product support teams.
Make a global impact by supporting product installations, training, and technical problem-solving at customer sites around the world.
Product Intelligence
Apply data science, AI, and machine learning to drive innovation in semiconductor equipment and process technology.
Develop predictive models that improve product performance, process stability, and manufacturing outcomes.
Utilize statistical analysis, experimentation, and hardware characterization data to optimize next-generation semiconductor solutions.
Communicate insights and recommendations across cross-functional teams to influence engineering and business decisions.
Field Process Engineer (FPE) – (ALD/EPI/PEALD/CVD)
Work directly with leading semiconductor companies to solve complex technical challenges, optimize processes, and enable the technology behind tomorrow’s chips.
Drive innovation from day one by supporting new product introductions, equipment installations, and process development for cutting-edge semiconductor manufacturing tools.
Collaborate with global teams across engineering, product management, sales, and customer support to deliver impactful technical solutions and accelerate technology adoption.
Build strong customer partnerships while identifying new opportunities, improving the user experience, and helping shape the future of semiconductor manufacturing.
