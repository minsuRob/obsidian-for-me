About this opportunity:
This opportunity is for a Marketing & Communications Intern at Ericsson Korea Partners. In this role, you will support internal and external marketing and communication activities while gaining hands-on experience in a global technology company. As an intern, you will work closely with the Marketing & Communications team to develop content and communication materials aligned with Ericsson’s brand guidelines.
This role offers a great opportunity to learn how marketing and communication activities are carried out in a multinational environment while contributing to initiatives that strengthen Ericsson’s brand and engagement in Korea. You will also have the opportunity to support communications related to innovative technologies such as 5G and next-generation network solutions.
What you will do:
Support internal and external marketing and communication activities
Support marketing material localization
Create graphics for internal communication materials such as PowerPoint slides, email banners, event announcements, and newsletters
Draft and edit digital and social media content (LinkedIn, X, YouTube, etc.)
Design and produce visual assets and marketing collateral based on Ericsson’s brand guidelines
Shoot and edit videos, and take photos for communication and documentation purposes
Proofread Korea-translated documents from English and publish them on internal and external web pages
Support the planning and operation of internal and external events such as employee meetings, customer seminars, and media briefings
Handle various administrative and coordination tasks related to marketing and communications
Requirements:
Prospective graduates or recent graduates with less than 1 year of work experience
Availability to work for at least 6 months or more
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English and Korean
Experience using MS Office tools (Excel, PowerPoint, Word) and Outlook
Interest in marketing, communications, and digital media
Basic video shooting/editing, design, or photography skills
Experience with Adobe tools such as Photoshop, Illustrator, or Premiere
Experience managing social media or blog content
A creative and innovative mindset with strong problem-solving ability
Quick learning ability and strong teamwork spirit
Other information you may want to know:
Location: Ericsson Korea Partners (Gangnam Meritz Tower)
Salary: KRW 10,320 per hour (before tax)
Type of Employment: Internship under a direct contract
Employment Period: 6 months from the start date (ASAP upon acceptance)
