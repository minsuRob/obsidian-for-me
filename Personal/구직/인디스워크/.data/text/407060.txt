[채용전환형 인턴] System Test Engineer
The System Hardware team covers the full lifecycle of NPU module development — from architecture design and board-level validation to mass production. Working with global ODM partners, the team ensures optimal performance and reliability. As an intern, you will gain hands-on experience in module bring-up, validation, failure analysis, and production setup.
Responsibilities and Opportunities
Assist senior test engineers in defining comprehensive test plans and performing integration, functional, and regression testing on hardware/software systems
Collaborate with automation engineers to develop test scripts while managing the configuration of test environments and hardware rigs
Investigate and document hardware defects, perform failure analysis, and report findings to internal teams
Work closely with developers and product managers to identify issues and improve overall product quality throughout the development lifecycle
Key Qualifications
Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field
Fundamental understanding of hardware systems including board-level components such as power, memory, and high-speed interfaces
Proficiency in scripting languages such as Python or shell scripting for test automation and data analysis
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills to identify, isolate, and debug complex technical issues
Excellent verbal and written communication skills for technical reporting and cross-functional collaboration
